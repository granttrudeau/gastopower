## Framing
- **Gas-to-power as a service is presented as an opportunity, not a current offer.** Research found no Baker Hughes-run power-as-a-service business today; Baker Hughes sells turbines, BRUSH generators and monitoring (iCenter, 1,800+ connected units) to power providers. On screen: "THE OPPORTUNITY · A VISION FOR UPSTREAM OIL & GAS"; end card: "A concept film about a future opportunity; not a commercial offer."
- "~85 MW" = 5 × 16.9 MWe (ISO); "≈34 MW" = 2 × 16.9 MWe.
- The 900-unit / 80-million-hour figure in the product sheet refers to the MS5002 heritage fleet, so the film does not attribute it to NovaLT.
- Technical figures match Baker Hughes' publicly posted NovaLT16 overview/datasheet (dam.bakerhughes.com, NovaLT16-Overview.pdf) and its 2017/2019 NovaLT brochures.
